# HR-Attrition-and-workforce-Analysis-Dashboard
-----------------------------------------------
## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition trends and workforce performance using HR analytics. The dashboard was developed to help management identify the key factors contributing to employee turnover and support data-driven HR decision-making.

The analysis focuses on understanding how department, job role, salary, satisfaction, overtime, work-life balance, and promotion history influence employee attrition.
---
## Business Problem

The company is experiencing increasing employee attrition and HR leadership wants to identify:

Which departments and job roles have the highest attrition
Whether salary, experience, or age impacts employee turnover
How job satisfaction and work-life balance affect retention
The relationship between overtime and employee exits
Which employees are at higher risk of leaving

## Key Insights
Operations and IT departments showed relatively higher attrition rates.
Employees with lower work-life balance and job satisfaction scores were more likely to leave.
Overtime appeared to have a strong relationship with employee attrition.
Employees with longer promotion gaps showed increased attrition risk.
Some high-performing employees were also leaving the organization, indicating possible retention issues.
---
## Recommendations
Conduct employee engagement and feedback sessions in high attrition departments.
Improve work-life balance policies and reduce excessive overtime.
Introduce transparent promotion and career growth plans.
Implement targeted retention strategies for high-risk employees.
Monitor employee satisfaction regularly through HR surveys and analytics.
